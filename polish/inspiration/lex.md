# Lex (added reference)

Category: Queer social / community app (text-first posts, groups, local meetups). Added because it is the closest queer-community peer: it shows how an LGBTQ+ app can carry identity without rainbow clichés, and where it falls short.

Why it feels premium (3-5 principles, each one sentence):
- Text-first posts with a big bold headline line in lowercase ("teach me 2 skate!", "art + coffee pals ☕") over two or three lines of body — the words are the content, like a personal ad.
- Identity comes from real photography of queer people and a bold, slightly retro flower/blob motif in flat green, yellow, purple on a deep navy ground (store art), not from rainbow stripes.
- Each post ends with one clear social action ("Chat") and plain tags ("Friends", "Group") — the next step is always a conversation.
- Groups are browsed by category tiles with real photos (Art, Food & Drink, Sports & Gym, Social Impact) and a "This week's most active" row.
- The voice is casual and in-group ("Find funsies right next to you", "If it's queer, it's here").

What it does with empty states:

Its signature interaction (the one thing people screenshot):
- The personal-ad style text post ("teach me 2 skate!") and the nearby map with avatars.

Its typography system (families, scale, weights):
- Observed: a heavy grotesk display for store headlines and post headlines (~20px bold lowercase), body ~14px regular, small meta ~12px grey ("New York, NY • Yesterday"), section heads ~15px semibold. Tag pills are pale tints with dark text.

Its motion language (durations, easing, what moves and what doesn't):
- Not observable in stills.

What we will NOT take from it:
- The lime-green bar chrome and the busy multi-hue tag colours (every chip a different pastel).
- The nearby map with member avatars and "Show me to everyone": location exposure is a safety risk for WLW members.
- Hookup-forward filters next to friendship ones.
