# Revolut

Category: Consumer banking / money super-app

Why it feels premium (3-5 principles, each one sentence):
- Pure black ground with surfaces separated by a lighter grey tone step and large radii, not by borders or shadows.
- Amounts are set big with small cents ("$5,325.80" with the ".80" at about half size), a typographic detail that reads crafted.
- The primary action is an inverted white pill with black text ("Send") beside a dark secondary ("Request") — primary by inversion, no brand fill needed.
- Payments live inside the chat as bubbles with a photo and an amount ("$25 For Lunch", screenshot 3) — the transaction becomes a social object.
- The card itself is the one place colour is allowed to be loud (the pink-to-violet card, the customised signature card, screenshot 4); the UI around it stays monochrome.

What it does on first run:
- Not shown in the store set ("Sign up in minutes" is the only claim).

What it does with empty states:
- Not shown in the store screenshots.

Its signature interaction (the one thing people screenshot):
- Customising the physical card (drawing a signature on it) and the savings balance over a photo background.

Its typography system (families, scale, weights):
- Observed: a geometric grotesk (Aeonik-like) for headlines at medium weight with tight leading; amounts semibold/bold, cents reduced; UI labels ~14px regular grey. From the popular-web-designs Revolut template: Aeonik Pro display at weight 500 with negative tracking, Inter for body, zero shadows, pills for all buttons, 8px base unit.

Its motion language (durations, easing, what moves and what doesn't):
- Not observable in stills. The 3D currency stack (screenshot 5) is marketing art, not app chrome.

What we will NOT take from it:
- The violet-pink gradient card (that is exactly the AI-glow family Roxy must leave).
- Finance density (Accounts, Interest paid, Earned this month tiles) — Roxy is not a dashboard.
