# HER (added reference)

Category: Lesbian, bi and queer dating + events app. Added because it is the direct WLW competitor members will compare Roxy with; it shows what "made for us" already looks like in the category.

Why it feels premium (3-5 principles, each one sentence):
- One deep owned field colour: every store screen sits on a dark oxblood/wine red with cream type, so the brand reads from across a room without a gradient.
- Real, candid, flash-lit photography of queer women of many presentations is the imagery — no illustration stands in for people.
- Identity words are the UI: outlined pill filters "Masc", "Nearby", "Femme", "T4T", and mood chips "Hookup Vibes", "Yearning", "Flirty" — in-group language set plainly.
- The event page is a poster first ("SAPPHIC SOCIAL CLUB" art), then title, a two-line date/place block, and two pills ("Tickets", "Going") — the Partiful pattern, in-category.
- Small sticker-like badges ("MASC" with a vest graphic) add play without colouring the chrome.

What it does with empty states:

Its signature interaction (the one thing people screenshot):
- Profile cards with identity stickers and the "Sent a note" moment.

Its typography system (families, scale, weights):
- Observed: store headlines in a bold rounded grotesk in cream; in-app a neutral grotesk — names "Mari, 28" ~20px bold white over the photo, meta lines ~12px with small icons, event title ~18px bold, body ~13px regular.

Its motion language (durations, easing, what moves and what doesn't):
- Not observable in stills.

What we will NOT take from it:
- Dating as the front door ("#1 Lesbian dating app", swipe hearts in the nav) — dating is opt-in in Roxy, never primary.
- Distance on every card ("2 miles") — location shown by default is an outing risk.
- The generic lilac "Going" button that ignores the brand field.
