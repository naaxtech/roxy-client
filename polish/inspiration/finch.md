# Finch

Category: Self-care companion (a pet bird that grows as you complete small goals)

Why it feels premium (3-5 principles, each one sentence):
- The companion carries the emotion so the UI does not have to: the bird hugs, cheers with pom-poms, holds a heart — every success has a character reaction rather than a badge.
- Goals are tiny and named in plain words ("Drink water", "Stretch", "Eat veggies", "Get out of bed", "Brush teeth") with a check box and a small energy count — kindness through scale, nothing is a big ask.
- Celebration is shared: "You & Sam are now Goal Buddies!" shows two columns (YOU / SAM) with each person's check, then one button, "Send encouragement" (screenshot 5).
- Sending care is an object: a card picker of illustrated stickers (Winter, Treat, Valentines, Outfit Love, Good Morning, Gratitude) and one "Send a Valentine!" button (screenshot 3).
- Headlines are short and warm in a heavy rounded face on a single pale sky field ("Self-care is better together").

What it does on first run:
- Not shown in the store set; the flow implied is adopting/naming the bird, then a first tiny goal list.

What it does with empty states:
- "3 goals left today!" with a "START THE DAY" group (screenshot 4) turns the remaining list into the state message; the pet's adventure timer ("back in 7:36") explains why the pet is away.

Its signature interaction (the one thing people screenshot):
- The bird (dressed, travelling, celebrating) and friend "Goal Buddies" moments.

Its typography system (families, scale, weights):
- Observed: a heavy rounded sans for headlines in deep navy, sentence case; in-app list items ~15px semibold, small group labels in tracked caps ("START THE DAY"), counts small. Two or three sizes on most screens.

Its motion language (durations, easing, what moves and what doesn't):
- Not observable in stills. Static cues: celebrations are character poses plus confetti in the scene; the list and buttons are plain.

What we will NOT take from it:
- Pastel sky-blue + candy-pink nursery palette and stuffed-toy characters; Roxy's members are adults and the app must read grown-up.
- Energy points and coin economies on every row.
- Tracked caps section labels.
