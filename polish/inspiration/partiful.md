# Partiful

Category: Event invites and RSVPs (social events, parties, meetups)

Why it feels premium (3-5 principles, each one sentence):
- The invite is a poster, not a form: each event page opens with a full-width, square-ish piece of art (a host-chosen or uploaded poster, e.g. "NOW THAT'S WHAT I CALL 26", "BIRTHDAY PARTY", "Book Club") with the title in a display face above it.
- The host picks a theme and effects, so every invite looks different while the chrome (title, date line, "Hosted by", address, RSVP) stays identical and quiet underneath.
- RSVP is one big round sticker-like button with an emoji state ("👍 Going") that floats over the poster corner — the answer feels like a gesture.
- Social proof is live and personal: small floating pills "Ben is Going 👍", "TJ is Interested ⭐", and stacked guest avatars with "+32 are Interested".
- Sharing is first-class and needs no app: "Share Link — Anyone with the link can RSVP" with the poster as the link preview card (screenshot 3).

What it does on first run:
- Not shown in the store set; the claim "The fastest, easiest event planning app" implies create-first. A guest's first run is the invite link itself (no app needed to RSVP).

What it does with empty states:
- Not shown in the store screenshots.

Its signature interaction (the one thing people screenshot):
- The invite page with its poster and the guest list, and the link preview it makes in group chats.

Its typography system (families, scale, weights):
- Observed: UI in a neutral grotesk (bold ~26px store headlines, ~15px body); event titles in host-chosen display faces (rounded heavy "lexi's 26th"); dates "Wed, January 22 / 9:30pm – 11:30pm" set as two stacked lines at medium weight. The variety lives inside the poster, never in the chrome.

Its motion language (durations, easing, what moves and what doesn't):
- Not observable in stills; screenshot 2 says invites have "animated themes, effects" — the motion is in the poster background (confetti, glitter), not in buttons or lists.

What we will NOT take from it:
- Public "Ben is Going" pills and public guest lists by default: for WLW events, who is going can out someone; attendance stays private unless a member chooses to show it.
- Neon violet/blue gradient theme defaults (the purple party glow).
- Event names in link previews by default (Roxy rule: share previews never show event names).
