# Raycast (iOS)

Category: Productivity launcher (notes, snippets, quicklinks, dictation on iPhone)

Why it feels premium (3-5 principles, each one sentence):
- Near-black ground with one hot accent (Raycast red) used only on the mark, the record button and checkboxes — colour marks the thing you act on.
- Dense lists that stay calm: one row height, small grey section labels by time ("Yesterday", "This Month"), a 16px icon per row, no cards inside cards.
- Physicality in small objects: the app icon and keycaps are rendered as tactile, glossy 3D objects, while the interface itself stays flat.
- Every surface is one tone step above the ground with a large radius, and floating panels (the composer, the record sheet) carry the only real shadow.
- Copy is short and functional, a plain sentence under a tiny label ("Speed through emails and messages with voice to text.").

What it does on first run:

What it does with empty states:

Its signature interaction (the one thing people screenshot):
- The red record button for dictation and the keyboard-first command composer ("Ask Raycast AI…").

Its typography system (families, scale, weights):
- Observed: Inter-like grotesk; headlines ~26px medium white; tiny tracked monospace labels above each headline ("AI CHAT", "DICTATION") in store art; list rows ~14px regular, section labels ~12px grey. From the popular-web-designs Raycast template: Inter everywhere with positive tracking on dark (+0.2px), weight 500 as the baseline, GeistMono for code only, 8px base unit, 6px "workhorse" radius on controls.

Its motion language (durations, easing, what moves and what doesn't):
- Not observable in stills; the template records multi-layer macOS-style shadows and pressed-key insets as the tactile language. Lists and text do not animate.

What we will NOT take from it:
- The tracked monospace eyebrow labels (banned by Taste Skill).
- The cold blue-black developer mood and the red-glow 3D renders.
- Anything that frames Roxy as an AI tool; Roxy is a wingwoman.
