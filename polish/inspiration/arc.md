# Arc (Arc Search, The Browser Company)

Category: Web browser / search (mobile: Arc Search)

Why it feels premium (3-5 principles, each one sentence):
- Strong, specific voice in every headline: "Summarize entire pages with a pinch — literally.", "Say goodbye to pop-ups and ads." — plain verbs, one wink, no feature-speak.
- Each feature has a physical gesture as its name (pinch to summarise, "Browse for Me"), so the interaction itself is the brand.
- Small honest receipts: "Scrubbed 6 ads and trackers" sits as a quiet card under the toggles (screenshot 5) — the app tells you what it did for you, in numbers.
- The chrome is a soft pale ground with one floating "+" bottom bar; the page content is shown full and untouched.
- Results are composed like an editorial page (a real painting at the top, a coloured serif-like title, short labelled paragraphs) rather than a list of links.

What it does on first run:
- Not shown in the store set; screenshot 1 implies the first screen is a search field with suggested queries each carrying a "Browse for Me" chip.

What it does with empty states:
- Incognito (screenshot 4) is an empty dark page with one quiet line, "You're browsing Incognito", and a ghost-feet icon in the field — the empty state is a mood change, not a lecture.

Its signature interaction (the one thing people screenshot):
- Pinch-to-summarise and "Browse for Me" building a page for you.

Its typography system (families, scale, weights):
- Observed: store headlines in a heavy rounded geometric sans in electric blue; in-app text in the system face (~15px body, bold titles), results titles in colour. (arc.net could not be captured: it redirects to a Notion page saying arc.net is unavailable in this region — arc-3.)

Its motion language (durations, easing, what moves and what doesn't):
- Not observable in stills. The Browser Company is known for haptic, springy small interactions; nothing here can be measured from screenshots.

What we will NOT take from it:
- The violet-to-pink gradient wash behind the app (screenshot 1) — the AI-glow family Roxy must leave.
- Electric blue + violet as brand colours.
