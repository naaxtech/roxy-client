# BeReal

Category: Photo sharing among friends (one daily prompt, one dual-camera photo)

Why it feels premium (3-5 principles, each one sentence):
- The interface is true black and white with almost no colour, so the only colour on screen is the member's own photo.
- One artifact, one shape: the dual-camera photo (big back-camera frame with a small rounded front-camera inset in the top-left corner) is identical everywhere — feed, send screen, calendar — so it is recognisable at thumbnail size.
- The chrome is tiny and centred: a bold wordmark ("BeReal."), two text tabs ("My Friends", "Friends of Friends"), a caption line; nothing competes with the photo.
- The rule is the product: "Unlock Hidden Pics by Posting Yours" (screenshot 5) blurs friends' posts with a single white "Post a BeReal." button until you share, so the gate is a design element, not a dialog.
- Memories are a plain calendar grid of your own photos with the date numeral on each day (screenshot 4) — your history is the decoration.

What it does on first run:
- Not shown in the store set. The single command on the capture screen is a large "SEND ➤" in white caps under the photo (screenshot 2): the first thing to do is post.

What it does with empty states:
- The locked feed is the empty state (screenshot 5): the friend's photo is blurred, an eye-slash icon, "Share to view / To view your friend's BeReal, post an update." and one white pill button. It tells you exactly what to do next.

Its signature interaction (the one thing people screenshot):
- The dual-camera photo itself (you + what you are looking at), and the "My BeReals" calendar of daily pictures.

Its typography system (families, scale, weights):
- Observed: one neo-grotesk; store headlines mix regular grey and bold white in the same line ("The Only App where people are 100% Real") — in-app the wordmark is bold, tabs ~14px semibold white/grey, captions ~13px regular, timestamps ~11px grey. Three sizes do the work.

Its motion language (durations, easing, what moves and what doesn't):
- Not observable in stills. Static cues: the inset photo is a separate rounded tile (it can be swapped by tapping); the blur over locked posts is a state, not an animation. Nothing decorative moves in the UI.

What we will NOT take from it:
- The daily notification panic ("2 min left") — Roxy must not pressure.
- Showing location or friends-of-friends by default: for WLW members that can out someone.
- The near-total absence of identity colour; Roxy keeps one owned brand colour.
