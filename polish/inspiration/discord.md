# Discord (added reference)

Category: Community chat (servers, text channels, voice hangouts). Added because Roxy communities are Discord-style channels; members who run communities already know this structure.

Why it feels premium (3-5 principles, each one sentence):
- The phone layout is a clear two-level map: a narrow server rail of rounded-square icons on the far left, the server's channel list beside it (Chat: "# main-chat", "# group-announcements"; "Voice Hangout": "gametime" with who is inside), and one green "Join Voice" pill at the bottom (screenshot 4).
- Presence is shown, not described: tiny coloured status dots on avatars, a speaker icon on the active voice channel, member names listed under it with mic/camera glyphs.
- Messages are a flat list (avatar, name in the member's role colour, time, text) with no bubbles, so long conversations stay readable.
- Status choices are four plain coloured circles (online, idle, do-not-disturb, invisible) in a small floating tray (screenshot 3) — colour carries meaning, one hue per state.
- Personality lives in user-made emoji and stickers and a few character illustrations, while the chrome stays grey on near-black.

What it does on first run:
- Not shown in the store set; the copy "No need to call or invite" / "Always something to do together" frames joining as dropping in.

What it does with empty states:
- Not shown in the store screenshots.

Its signature interaction (the one thing people screenshot):
- Custom emoji and the voice channel showing who is hanging out right now.

Its typography system (families, scale, weights):
- Observed: store headlines in a heavy condensed display in caps (Discord's ABC Ginto Nord) — the in-app UI uses a neutral grotesk (gg sans): channel names ~15px medium with "#", usernames ~15px semibold in role colours, message text ~15px regular, timestamps ~12px grey, section labels ~12px grey.

Its motion language (durations, easing, what moves and what doesn't):
- Not observable in stills. Static cues: speaking members get a green ring; the voice tray slides up from the bottom.

What we will NOT take from it:
- Blurple (#5865F2-family indigo) and the violet store backgrounds — the AI-glow hue family.
- The server rail as a permanent column on a phone; Roxy keeps one region at a time.
- Gamer density and caps display in UI.
