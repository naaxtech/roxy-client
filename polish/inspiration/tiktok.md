# TikTok (added reference)

Category: Short-video vertical feed. Added because Roxy's Feed is a TikTok-style pager; this is the grammar her members already have in their thumbs.

Why it feels premium (3-5 principles, each one sentence):
- The content owns 100% of the screen and the chrome is thin white glyphs with a soft shadow, no plates or boxes — the rail is ~5 icons (avatar with a red "+", heart, comment, save, share) with small counts under each (991K, 3456, 1256).
- Text on the video is the creator's, set big and bold in the middle ("What exactly is a black hole?"), while the app's own caption is small and bottom-left (@handle, one line, the sound line with a music note).
- The top bar is just two words, "Following  For You", the active one white and bold, the other dimmed, plus a LIVE glyph left and a search glyph right.
- One accent colour pair (cyan/red offset) appears only on the logo and the "+" create button in the bottom bar; the nav itself is black with white icons and tiny labels.
- Spacing on the rail is generous (~56px pitch) so the thumb never hits the wrong control.

What it does on first run:
- Not shown in the store set; publicly known behaviour is that it plays a video immediately with no sign-up wall.

What it does with empty states:
- Not shown in the store screenshots.

Its signature interaction (the one thing people screenshot):
- The full-screen video itself with its rail, and the double-tap heart.

Its typography system (families, scale, weights):
- Observed: a proprietary geometric sans (TikTok Sans) — top tabs ~16px semibold, handle ~15px bold, caption ~14px regular, counts ~11px semibold; creator overlay text is the only large type. Store headlines bold ~24px.

Its motion language (durations, easing, what moves and what doesn't):
- Not observable in stills. Publicly known: vertical snap paging, a heart burst on double tap, a spinning sound disc bottom-right. The chrome itself never moves.

What we will NOT take from it:
- The infinite, attention-maximising loop with no end; Roxy's feed should let her finish ("You're caught up").
- Public follower/like vanity counts as the loudest thing on a post.
- AI-generated artwork tools as a feature.
